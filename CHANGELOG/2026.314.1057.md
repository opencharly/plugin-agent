## Summary

Convert the five inline `|` word unions in `candy/plugin-agent/schema/agent.cue` to the
§10.1 S3 vocabulary-table idiom, so each closed set of words has ONE home and the enum an
author meets is `or()` of that table's keys:

```
_mode  _evidencePolicy  _transport  _persistence  _transcript
       (+ their derived _…Word lists)
```

That is the 5 `union` rows `design/dpat-baseline.tsv` records for this file — **five
single-line disjunctions across five definitions**, every one a pure word list:

| table | words | field | def |
|---|---|---|---|
| `_mode` | 2 | `mode?` | `#AgCredentialMount` |
| `_evidencePolicy` | 3 | `evidence_policy?` | `#AgentTeamInput` |
| `_transport` | 5 | `transport` | `#AtTargetHop` |
| `_persistence` | 3 | `persistence?` | `#AtTerminalProfile` |
| `_transcript` | 4 | `transcript?` | `#AtTerminalProfile` |

No default, no scalar-type alternative, no heterogeneous or struct alternative among them.
Every row this repo carried is retired by this PR; no row is left to judgement.

**The two literal defaults this schema already carries are NOT touched.** `prompt_via:
*"argv" | "file"` and `output_format: *"" | "stream-json"` are not unions written out
alternative by alternative — S3 permits a literal default inline, and
`scripts/design-dpat.sh`'s `literal_default` exempts exactly that shape — so they stay
exactly as they were, and `TestS3VocabularyTablesDriveTheirEnums`' sibling in
`schema_served_test.go` pins both through the served schema. §7 accounts for every `|`
left in the file, including those two, the two `cols`/`rows` defaults, the `#AgDuration`
regex (a `|` inside a string, not a union) and one comment that quotes them.

The accepted sets are IDENTICAL before and after — 2 / 3 / 5 / 3 / 4 words, read from the
compiled CUE on both sides (185-case differential in §4).

**The generated projection does not move at all.** `charly candy params plugin-agent
--check` says `current` on the pristine tree and again after the sweep, so this diff
carries no `params/cue_types_gen.go` change: every generated type, field and tag is
byte-identical.
